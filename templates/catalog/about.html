About

Started on a
balcony in Bengaluru.

Two friends, one sewing machine, and a shared grievance: the nice kaftans cost more than the flight, and the cheap ones didn't survive the first wash.

What we make

Resort wear, which is a fancy way of saying clothes for the good days. Kaftans you can throw on over a swimsuit or a hangover. Cover-ups that are honest about being sheer. Dresses that go from breakfast buffet to sunset bar. Co-ord and shorts sets that make you look like you packed properly.

Everything is cotton or a cotton blend, because it's 34 degrees and nobody needs polyester in their life. Prints are hand-screened in our two colours: a blue we argued about for three weeks and a cream we didn't.

How we make it

Small batches. Ten to twenty pieces per style, cut and sewn by a four-person unit in Bengaluru that we visit every week and pay properly. We don't do pre-orders and we don't restock most things. When it's gone, we cut something new.

Prices sit between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000, on purpose. We're for college students and first-job people, not for the resort boutique markup. Prepaid only, no cash on delivery, so we're not paying for a hundred returned parcels and passing it on to you.

The name

Bay, as in the water. Bae, as in you. That's it. We're not going to pretend there's a deeper meaning; the domain was free.
