How to measure

Bust: around the fullest part, tape flat, breathing normally. Waist: the narrowest point, usually just above the belly button. Hip: the widest part of your hips and bum, feet together.

No tape? Use a phone charger cable and a ruler. We've all done it.

By style

Kaftans are one size, cut 60 inches wide at the sleeve and 120 to 124 cm long. They fit everyone from UK 6 to 16; on shorter frames they sit closer to the ankle.

Cover-ups are one size and open at the front, so fit is about length: the Sheer Audacity sits above the knee, the Beach Please sits mid-calf.

Slip dresses are bias-cut and fitted through the bust. Size on your bust measurement. Tiered dresses are fitted at the bodice and waist; size on your waist.

Co-ords and shorts sets are relaxed. The pants and shorts have an elasticated back waist, so size on your hip.
