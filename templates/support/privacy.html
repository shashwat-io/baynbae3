Policy

Privacy

We collect what we need to send you a parcel and nothing that we'd be embarrassed to explain.

What we collect

When you order: your name, email, phone number and delivery address. When you write to us: whatever you put in the form. That's the list.

What we don't

Card numbers, UPI IDs, bank details. Payment happens on Razorpay's page and they send us a reference number, not your details. We don't run accounts, so there's no password to leak. We don't use advertising trackers.

Cookies

One session cookie, so your bag survives between pages, and one CSRF cookie, so nobody can submit forms pretending to be you. Both are essential and neither identifies you to anyone else.

Who sees it

Us, Razorpay (payment), and the courier (delivery address and phone). Nobody else, and we don't sell or rent anything.

How long

Order records are kept for as long as Indian tax law makes us keep them. Support messages are kept until resolved and then for a year in case it comes up again. Ask us to delete your details and we will, unless the law says otherwise.
