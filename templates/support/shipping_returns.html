Policy

Shipping & exchanges

Short version: free shipping over ₹{{ store.free_shipping_over_inr|intcomma }}, dispatch in {{ store.dispatch_days }}, exchanges within 7 days, refunds only for defects.

Shipping

Orders are packed at our Bengaluru studio and dispatched within {{ store.dispatch_days }} of payment. Shipping is free on orders of ₹{{ store.free_shipping_over_inr|intcomma }} and above, and ₹{{ store.shipping_flat_inr }} below that.

Delivery usually takes 2–3 days to metros and 4–7 days elsewhere in India after dispatch. We'll add the courier and tracking number to your order the day it leaves; find it on the Track page. We don't ship outside India yet.

Hostel or PG? Put the gate timings or the warden's name in the note at checkout and we pass it to the courier.

Payment

All orders are prepaid via Razorpay: UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. There is no cash on delivery. Your card and UPI details go to Razorpay directly; we never see them.

Exchanges

You can exchange for a different size within 7 days of delivery, provided the piece is unworn, unwashed, with tags attached, and we still have the size. Write to us from the Contact page with your order number and we'll arrange a pickup. One exchange per order. If the size you want is gone, we'll offer a store credit.

Defects & refunds

Every piece is checked before it goes out, but if something arrives damaged or wrong, send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace it or refund you in full, your choice. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

We don't offer refunds for change of mind. Small batches don't survive a return culture, and keeping returns low is a big part of why the prices are what they are.

Cancellations

Changed your mind within a few hours? Message us and if it hasn't been packed we'll cancel and refund in full. Once it's packed, it's an exchange.
