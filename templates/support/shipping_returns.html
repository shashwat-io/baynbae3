Shipping

Orders are packed in {% owner "dispatch city" %} and dispatched within {% owner "dispatch window" %} of payment. {{ store.shipping_note }}

Delivery usually takes {% owner "delivery window" %} after dispatch. We'll add the courier and tracking number to your order the day it leaves; find it on the Track page. We don't ship outside India yet.

Hostel or PG? Put the gate timings or the warden's name in the note at checkout and we pass it to the courier.

Payment

All orders are prepaid via Razorpay: UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. There is no cash on delivery. Your card and UPI details go to Razorpay directly; we never see them.

Returns

Returns are for defects only. If something arrives damaged or wrong, write to us from the Contact page within 3–5 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll replace it or refund you in full, your choice. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

There are no returns or exchanges for change of mind or fit, so check the size guide before you order. Small batches don't survive a return culture, and keeping returns low is a big part of why the prices are what they are.

Cancellations

Changed your mind within a few hours? Message us and if it hasn't been packed we'll cancel and refund in full. Once it's packed, it's on its way.
